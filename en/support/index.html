Plus: buying, restoring and codes

  • A purchase belongs to your Apple Account, not to the phone. On a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple Account, Plus comes back by itself.
  • If it doesn't: Settings → Plus → Restore Purchases. The App Store may ask for your Apple Account password.
  • Enter a promo code in Settings → Plus → Redeem Code, or in the App Store: your profile picture → Redeem Gift Card or Code.
  • Plus for a year is an auto-renewable subscription. Turn off auto-renewal at least 24 hours before the period ends in Settings → Plus → Manage Subscription, or in the iPhone's Settings: your name → Subscriptions. Plus keeps working until the end of the paid period.
  • Both purchases also work for your family if you share purchases with Family Sharing.
  • Payments and refunds are handled by Apple: ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. I never see your payment details.

Backups

Your logbook, photos and spots are only on your iPhone, with no account or server. Deleting the app deletes them from the phone. Make a backup before deleting the app or changing phones.

  • A backup now: Settings → Your Data → Export Backup (JSON + Photos), and save the folder in Files, ideally on iCloud Drive.
  • Automatic backups: Settings → Automatic Backup → Turn On Automatic Backup… and choose a folder, e.g. on iCloud Drive. The next backups save themselves when you open the app (daily or weekly); the 7 newest are kept.
  • Backup password: Settings → Your Data → Backup Password encrypts every backup. Keep the password somewhere safe: without it a backup can't be opened, and I can't recover it.
  • Restoring: Settings → Your Data → Import Backup, then choose the backup's folder. Importing adds only what's missing, so the same backup can be imported twice without duplicates.
  • A new iPhone: make a backup on iCloud Drive, install RyboLog on the new phone and import it.
