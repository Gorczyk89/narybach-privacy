2. What the app does

Na rybach is a fishing logbook for iPhone: you record trips, catches, spots and your own rules in it, and the app adds the weather, works out statistics and suggests when to go. The logbook is only on your iPhone, with no account or server.

The weather, aerial photos and maps come straight to your iPhone from their providers: the weather from MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) and NASA POWER (NASA), aerial photos of spots in Poland from the Polish Head Office of Geodesy and Cartography (Geoportal), and the maps from Apple. They work independently of me and aren’t affiliated with me.

7. Way Back

“Way Back” shows an arrow and the distance to a saved place (a spot, a point in a spot or where a fish was caught), from GPS and the compass, without internet. Direction and distance are approximate: GPS and the compass can be wrong. On the water, use your own judgement.
